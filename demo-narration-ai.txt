Here’s the Stock Research CLI. It reviews a portfolio and watchlist against a market snapshot you provide. The sample companies and numbers on screen are fictional.

The portfolio section calculates position values and gains or losses from the share counts, cost basis, and prices in the snapshot. The watchlist appears alongside your holdings.

The emerging-growth and established-company screens show the metrics behind each result. Buy review and sell review flags are reminders to check the evidence. They are not trade instructions.

Add the AI brief option for a second-pass summary of the supplied data. It points out patterns and missing information, and doesn’t fetch live prices. Without that option, the CLI runs offline.

Check the source, date, and assumptions before using any of the results. There’s no brokerage connection and no order execution.
