Scene 1
This is a headless stock research program. It reviews your portfolio and watchlist using a dated market snapshot. It cannot connect to a brokerage or place trades.

Scene 2
Here are the positions. Values and gains or losses use only the prices and cost basis you provide. Missing data stays blank. Your watchlist appears below.

Scene 3
The emerging stock screen looks for rising revenue, strong twenty day momentum, and unusual trading volume. The established company screen looks for larger businesses with lower volatility, positive free cash flow, and manageable debt. These filters do not guarantee safety.

Scene 4
The buy review and sell review are prompts to inspect the evidence, not instructions to trade. This demo uses fictional data. For real analysis, provide a current market snapshot with its source and date.
